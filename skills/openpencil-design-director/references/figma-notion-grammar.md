# Figma and Notion visual grammar

Use these references as interaction and composition guidance, not as branding to copy.

## Figma and FigJam

- Infinite canvas supports exploration, but sections create navigable islands.
- A section should have one workshop, review, comparison, or narrative purpose.
- Pages or widely separated sections prevent oversized boards and loading/attention problems.
- Large artifacts and short annotations beat dense miniature reconstructions.
- Tidy alignment and repeated spacing communicate structure before labels.
- Reference: https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500004362321-Guide-to-FigJam
- Reference: https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/4939765379351-Organize-your-FigJam-board-with-sections
- Reference: https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/24005082123159-Create-and-manage-pages-in-FigJam

## Notion

- One object can appear through multiple views without duplicating its identity.
- Each view controls visible properties, grouping, filtering, and sorting.
- Detailed pages open in side peek, center peek, or full page so the collection remains usable.
- Documents use sparse hierarchy and readable line length rather than dashboard cards for every paragraph.
- Linked views bring relevant subsets of knowledge into context.
- Reference: https://www.notion.com/help/views-filters-and-sorts
- Reference: https://www.notion.com/help/notion-academy/lesson/linked-views

## OpenPencil synthesis

- Use the canvas for spatial relationship and design review.
- Use focused knowledge/detail views for prose and records.
- Keep one shared object identity across projections.
- Make a selected application state large enough to review.
- Keep lifecycle and source-safety states explicit but visually subordinate.
